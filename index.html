    About

    Redshift Motel is a three-piece instrumental rock band from Austin, Texas, playing music from the last bar at the edge of the galaxy. Their sound is riff-driven and cinematic — desert rock run through tape delay, surf reverb pointed at deep space — and every song is a scene at the motel: a guest checking in from three light-years out, an ice machine humming at 3 AM, a transmission nobody can trace.

    No vocals, no costumes. Just a working bar band playing a real set for anyone who took the wrong exit off the Milky Way.
